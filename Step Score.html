Challenges & stakes

Stakes multiply the points a challenge is worth. Change a number and everything recalculates.

How points work

Per challenge: 1 for entering, up to 6 more for placement (1st = full, last = 0), and win/podium bonuses (+3 / +1.5 / +0.8). Winning teams share a bonus pool, split by each member’s share of the team’s steps, so the biggest contributor gets the most. Everything is multiplied by the challenge’s stakes and a small field-size factor, so beating more people is worth more.

Monthly rating

Your recent stepping, UTR-style. Each month your daily average is compared with the group’s, and recent months count most. 5.0 = group average. Tap a name for their months.

Add or update a month

Saving a month that already exists replaces it, so you can re-paste the current month as it grows. For a partial month, set days counted to the days so far.

Recency

Last month counts 70% as much as this month, the month before 49%, and so on. A month someone has no data for is skipped for them.

Medals

🥇 1st place · 🎖️ team win · 🥈 2nd place. One medal per finish, no points.
